How it works

Four steps, then it repeats every month.

You stay in charge of the live game. We never take passwords, and we never publish a patch unless you say yes.

You point us at the game

Store links, a collaborator invite to the repo, and optionally Discord. You can revoke access at any time.

We watch the reviews

AI helps gather and sort player feedback. You get a digest and a short recommended patch list.

We patch what’s broken

Fixes and small balance tweaks inside your plan’s hours. Extra work only happens if you approve it first.

A human checks it

Every patch is reviewed, then you approve anything that will go live. You keep the store accounts.

Common questions

Before you write to us.

Are the free ThinkyBuildy tools staying free?

Yes. The calculators, charts, and other tools on thinkybuildy.com stay free, with no ads and no sign-ups. Game Care is a separate paid service for people who have shipped a game and want help after launch.

What does a month actually include?

A regular review digest, a short patch list, and a capped number of hours for fixes and light balance work: 4 hours on Hobbyist, 12 hours on Earning. Unused hours do not roll over. Nothing goes to a live game without your okay.

Will you need my store passwords?

No. Never send passwords, two-factor codes, or recovery keys. A collaborator invite to the repo, and a role you can revoke, is enough. You keep the store accounts.

Do you take a cut of the game, or own any of it?

No. You own the game, the code, and the work we create for you. We do not take a revenue share.

Can you guarantee better reviews or more sales?

No, and we will not pretend to. We can watch the feedback and ship careful fixes. Players, stores, and discovery are still out of anyone’s full control.

What engines and tools do you work with?

Unity, Godot, Unreal, and web games, including projects built with AI coding tools. If a project is too fragile to touch safely in the included hours, we will say so and may suggest a one-time stabilization quote instead of a monthly plan.

How do I cancel?

Anytime, by email. The plan runs through the end of the month already paid. There is no long-term contract.
