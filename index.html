The toolbox

Eight tools, all live, all free.

Each one does one job well. Tap a card to open it.

Date Calculator

Days between dates, add or subtract time, exact age, business days with U.S. federal holidays, and live countdowns.

dayspan.pages.dev

Tire Size Calculator

Compare two tire sizes: diameter, width, sidewall, revolutions per mile, speedometer error and a to-scale drawing.

tiresizing.pages.dev

Cups & Quarts

A cooking converter for cups, spoons, grams and ounces, cups to grams from USDA data, oven temperatures and pan sizes.

cupsnquarts.pages.dev

BIM Reference Library

A guide to BIM software and reality-capture hardware, from Revit add-ins and coordination to scanning and drones, with cited ratings.

bimchart.net

Ammo Reference Library

A field reference for hunters and shooters: cartridge, caliber, rifle and optics charts, cartridge profiles and head-to-head comparisons.

ammochart.net

Live Power Outage Map

An unofficial live map of major power outages worldwide, with customers affected, causes, restoration times and sources.

blackoutsmap.com

Well Pump Reference Library

A cross-brand reference for residential and light-ag well pumps, pressure tanks and controls, with a compare tool and sizing basics.

wellpumpchart.pages.dev

Safety Tool Chart

A cross-brand fall protection reference: harnesses, lanyards, SRLs, anchors and rescue gear compared, with OSHA/ANSI basics and a compare tool.

safetytoolchart.pages.dev

How it's built

One agent, one job.

Every tool goes through the same small crew. The agents do the busywork, and nothing goes live until a human has checked it.

Finds the questions people keep searching for and checks we're not copying anyone.

Builder

Writes the site from a spec and puts it on fast, static hosting.

Fact-check

Re-does the math and checks the sources before anything ships.

Human

Reviews the changes, tests on a phone, and approves every release.
